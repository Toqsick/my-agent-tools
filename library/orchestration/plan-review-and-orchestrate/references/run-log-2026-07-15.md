## Lessons-Learned (for next iteration)

1. **Run Phase A always.** 5 Schwächen categories caught 2 IDs-Placeholder bugs
   that would have crashed every downstream task.
2. **Queen-Verify every subagent output.** Even "PASS" reports can be wrong
   (Pitfall #36 variants a/b/c).
3. **Mode-Switch on Crash-Rate.** >20% crashes → drop reviewers, rely on
   Queen-Verify.
4. **Mnemosyne-Triples for relationships.** 4 triples (Section-Relationships)
   make future recall much sharper than 7 individual lessons.
5. **Side-Effects as Discoveries.** Hygiene-Snippet revealed 781 missing
   frontmatter fields — unplanned value-add.
