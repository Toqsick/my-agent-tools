## Two-System Architecture: CORE + EEAT

### CORE — GEO (AI Engine Optimization)
Evaluates content body for AI citation potential:
- **C (Contextual Clarity)**: Does the content clearly answer the user's intent?
- **O (Organization)**: Is the content structured for both humans and machines?
- **R (Referenceability)**: Can AI and readers verify and cite the claims?
- **E (Exclusivity)**: Does the content offer something unavailable elsewhere?

### EEAT — SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Evaluates source credibility:
- **Exp (Experience)**: Does the author demonstrate real-world experience?
- **Ept (Expertise)**: Does the author demonstrate professional expertise?
- **A (Authority)**: Is the author/org recognized as an authority?
- **T (Trust)**: Does the site meet trust and safety standards?

## Workflow: Keyword Research

Use when the user asks to "find keywords", "keyword research", "what should I write about", "identify ranking opportunities", or "topic ideas".

### 1. Understand the Context
Ask clarifying questions if not provided:
- What is your product/service/topic?
- Who is your target audience?
- What is your business goal? (traffic, leads, sales)
- What is your current domain authority?
- Any specific geographic targeting?

### 2. Generate Seed Keywords
Start with:
- Core product/service terms
- Problem-focused keywords
- Solution-focused keywords
- Audience-specific terms
- Industry terminology

### 3. Expand Keyword List

**Modifiers:**
- Best [keyword]
- Top [keyword]
- [keyword] for [audience]
- How to [keyword]
- What is [keyword]
- [keyword] vs [alternative]
- [keyword] examples
- [keyword] tools

**Long-tail Variations:**
- [keyword] for beginners
- [keyword] for small business
- Free [keyword]
- [keyword] template
- [keyword] checklist

### 4. Classify Search Intent

| Intent | Signals | Content Type |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| Informational | what, how, why, guide | Blog posts, guides |
| Navigational | brand names, login | Homepage, product pages |
| Commercial | best, review, vs, compare | Comparison posts, reviews |
| Transactional | buy, price, discount | Product pages, pricing |

### 5. Assess Keyword Difficulty

**High Difficulty (70-100):**
- Major brands ranking
- High domain authority competitors
- Established content (1000+ backlinks)

**Medium Difficulty (40-69):**
- Mix of authority and niche sites
- Moderate backlink requirements

**Low Difficulty (1-39):**
- Few authoritative competitors
- Thin or outdated content ranking
- New or emerging topics

### 6. Calculate Opportunity Score

Formula: `Opportunity = (Volume × Intent Value) / Difficulty`

Intent Values:
- Informational = 1
- Navigational = 1
- Commercial = 2
- Transactional = 3

### 7. Identify GEO Opportunities

**High GEO Potential Keywords:**
- Question formats: "What is...", "How does..."
- Definition queries: "[term] meaning"
- Comparison queries: "[A] vs [B]"
- List queries: "best [category]"
- How-to queries: "how to [action]"

### 8. Create Topic Clusters

**Pillar Content**: Broad primary keyword (comprehensive guide)
**Cluster Content**: Specific long-tail keywords linking to pillar

### 9. Output Report

Include:
- Executive summary
- Top keyword opportunities (quick wins, growth, GEO)
- Topic clusters with pillar-cluster relationships
- Content calendar recommendations
- Next steps

## Workflow: SEO Content Writing

Use when the user asks to "write SEO content", "create a blog post", "write an article", or "draft optimized content".

### 1. Gather Requirements

**Content Requirements:**
- Primary Keyword
- Secondary Keywords (2-5)
- Target Word Count
- Content Type (blog/guide/landing page)
- Target Audience
- Search Intent
- Tone
- CTA Goal

### 2. Load CORE-EEAT Quality Constraints

Apply these standards while writing:
- C01: Intent Alignment (title promise = content delivery)
- C02: Direct Answer (core answer in first 150 words)
- C06: Audience Targeting (state "this article is for...")
- O01: Heading Hierarchy (H1→H2→H3, no level skipping)
- O02: Summary Box (include TL;DR or Key Takeaways)
- R01: Data Precision (≥5 precise numbers with units)
- R02: Citation Density (≥1 external citation per 500 words)

### 3. Create Optimized Title

Requirements:
- Include primary keyword (preferably at start)
- Under 60 characters
- Compelling and click-worthy
- Match search intent

### 4. Write Meta Description

Requirements:
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Include call-to-action
- Compelling and specific

### 5. Structure Content

```
# [H1 with Primary Keyword]

[Hook + Problem + Promise + Overview]

## [H2 - First Main Section with Secondary Keyword]
[Content with examples and evidence]

### [H3 - Sub-section if needed]
[Detailed content with bullet points]

## [H2 - Second Main Section]
[Continue with valuable content]

## Frequently Asked Questions

### [Question from common query]?
[Direct answer in 40-60 words for featured snippet]

## Conclusion
[Summary + Final insight + Clear CTA]
```

### 6. Apply On-Page SEO Best Practices

**Keyword Placement:**
- [ ] Primary keyword in title
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Primary keyword in at least one H2
- [ ] Primary keyword in conclusion
- [ ] Secondary keywords in H2s/H3s

**Content Quality:**
- [ ] Comprehensive coverage of topic
- [ ] Original insights or data
- [ ] Actionable takeaways
- [ ] Examples and illustrations
- [ ] Expert quotes or citations

**Readability:**
- [ ] Paragraphs of 3-5 sentences
- [ ] Bullet points and lists
- [ ] Bold key phrases
- [ ] Table of contents for long content

### 7. Optimize for Featured Snippets

**Definition Snippets:** Clear definition in 40-60 words
**List Snippets:** Numbered or bulleted lists under H2s
**Table Snippets:** Comparison tables with clear headers
**How-To Snippets:** Numbered steps: "Step 1:", "Step 2:"

### 8. Add Links

**Internal Links (2-5):**
Link to relevant pages with descriptive anchor text

**External Links (2-3):**
Link to authoritative sources supporting claims

### 9. Final SEO Review

Score the content on:
- Title optimization
- Meta description
- H1 with keyword
- Keyword in first 100 words
- H2s optimization
- Internal/external links
- FAQ section
- Readability
- Word count

## Workflow: GEO Content Optimization

Use when the user asks to "optimize for AI", "get cited by ChatGPT", "AI optimization", "appear in AI answers", or "make content AI-quotable".

### How AI Systems Select Content to Cite

AI systems prioritize content that is:

**Authoritative:**
- From recognized experts or trusted sources
- Contains proper citations and references
- Shows expertise signals

**Accurate:**
- Factually correct and verifiable
- Up-to-date information
- Consistent with consensus knowledge

**Clear:**
- Well-structured and organized
- Contains clear definitions and explanations
- Uses unambiguous language

**Quotable:**
- Has standalone statements that answer questions
- Contains specific facts, statistics, and data
- Includes memorable, concise explanations

### 1. Analyze Current Content

Score 1-10 on:
- Clear definitions
- Quotable statements
- Factual density
- Source citations
- Q&A format
- Authority signals
- Content freshness
- Structure clarity

### 2. Optimize Definitions

**Before (Weak):**
> SEO is really important for businesses and involves various techniques to improve visibility online.

**After (Strong):**
> **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** is the practice of optimizing websites and content to rank higher in search engine results pages (SERPs), increasing organic traffic and visibility.

**Definition Template:**
"[Term] is [clear category] that [primary function], [key characteristic]."

### 3. Create Quotable Statements

**Types of Quotable Statements:**

1. **Statistics:** Include specific numbers with sources
   "According to [Source], [specific statistic] as of [date]."

2. **Facts:** Verifiable information from authoritative sources
   "[Subject] was [fact], according to [source]."

3. **Comparisons:** Clear comparison structure
   "Unlike [A], [B] [specific difference], which means [implication]."

4. **How-to Steps:** Numbered, clear steps
   "To [achieve goal], [step 1], then [step 2], and finally [step 3]."

### 4. Add Authority Signals

- Expert quotes with credentials
- Citations to peer-reviewed research
- Original data or research
- Case studies with named companies

### 5. Optimize Content Structure

**Q&A Format:**
```html
<h2>What is [Topic]?</h2>
<p>[Direct answer in 40-60 words]</p>

<h2>How does [Topic] work?</h2>
<p>[Clear explanation with steps]</p>
```

**Comparison Tables:**
| Feature | Option A | Option B |
|---------|----------|----------|
| [Feature] | [Value] | [Value] |
| **Best for** | [Use case] | [Use case] |

### 6. Enhance Factual Density

**Low factual density:**
> Social media marketing is very popular nowadays.

**High factual density:**
> Social media marketing reaches 4.9 billion users globally (Statista, 2024). Businesses using social media marketing report 66% higher lead generation rates compared to non-users (HubSpot, 2024).

### 7. Implement FAQ Schema

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "[Question text]",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "[Answer text]"
    }
  }]
}
```

### 8. GEO Readiness Checklist

**Definitions & Clarity:**
- [ ] Key terms are clearly defined
- [ ] Definitions can stand alone as answers
- [ ] Language is precise and unambiguous

**Quotable Content:**
- [ ] Specific statistics included
- [ ] Facts have source citations
- [ ] Memorable statements created

**Authority:**
- [ ] Expert quotes or credentials present
- [ ] Authoritative sources cited
- [ ] Original data or research included

**Structure:**
- [ ] Q&A format sections included
- [ ] Clear headings match common queries
- [ ] Comparison tables where relevant

**Technical:**
- [ ] FAQ schema markup added
- [ ] Content freshness indicated
- [ ] Sources are verifiable

## Workflow: Content Quality Audit (CORE-EEAT)

Use when the user asks to "audit content quality", "EEAT score", "content quality check", "CORE-EEAT audit", or "is my content good enough to rank". Runs a full 80-item CORE-EEAT content quality audit with weighted scoring.

### Scoring

#### Per-Item Scoring
- **Pass** = 10 points
- **Partial** = 5 points
- **Fail** = 0 points

#### Score Calculation
- **GEO Score** = (C + O + R + E) / 4
- **SEO Score** = (Exp + Ept + A + T) / 4
- **Weighted Score** = Σ (dimension_score × content_type_weight)

#### Veto Items (Override Score)
- **T04**: Affiliate links without disclosure
- **C01**: Clickbait (title doesn't match content)
- **R10**: Data contradicts itself

### Content-Type Weight Table

| Dim | Product Review | How-to | Comparison | Blog Post |
|-----|---------------|--------|------------|-----------|
| C | 10% | 20% | 10% | 25% |
| O | 10% | 20% | 20% | 10% |
| R | 15% | 10% | 25% | 10% |
| E | 20% | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| Exp | 20% | 5% | 5% | 10% |
| Ept | 5% | 20% | 15% | 10% |
| A | 5% | 5% | 5% | 5% |
| T | 15% | 15% | 10% | 10% |

### Key Check Items

#### CORE — Content Body

**C (Contextual Clarity):**
- C01: Intent Alignment — Title promise = content delivery
- C02: Direct Answer — Core answer in first 150 words
- C09: FAQ Coverage — Structured FAQ with Schema
- C10: Semantic Closure — Conclusion answers opening

**O (Organization):**
- O01: Heading Hierarchy — H1→H2→H3, no skipping
- O02: Summary Box — TL;DR or Key Takeaways
- O03: Data Tables — Comparisons in tables
- O05: Schema Markup — Appropriate JSON-LD

**R (Referenceability):**
- R01: Data Precision — ≥5 precise numbers with units
- R02: Citation Density — ≥1 per 500 words
- R04: Evidence-Claim Mapping — Claims backed by evidence
- R06: Timestamp & Versioning — Updated <1 year

**E (Exclusivity):**
- E01: Original Data — First-party data
- E02: Novel Framework — Named, citable framework
- E07: Practical Tools — Downloadable templates/checklists

#### EEAT — Source Credibility

**Exp (Experience):**
- Exp01: First-Person Narrative — "I tested" or "We found"
- Exp04: Tangible Proof — Original photos with timestamps
- Exp06: Problems Encountered — Real problems + solutions

**Ept (Expertise):**
- Ept01: Author Identity — Byline + avatar + bio
- Ept02: Credentials Display — Relevant qualifications
- Ept10: Editorial Process — "Reviewed by" labels

**A (Authority):**
- A01: Backlink Profile — Cited by authoritative sites
- A07: Knowledge Graph Presence — Wikipedia/Knowledge Panel

**T (Trust):**
- T01: Legal Compliance — Privacy Policy + TOS
- T04: Disclosure Statements — Affiliate links disclosed
- T08: Risk Disclaimers — YMYL topics have disclaimers

### Content Quality Audit Output Structure

1. **Overview** — Content, type, date, total score, veto status
2. **Dimension Scores** — Each dimension with rating and weight
3. **Per-Item Scores** — All 80 items with Pass/Partial/Fail
4. **Top 5 Priority Improvements** — Sorted by weighted impact
5. **Action Plan** — Quick wins, medium effort, strategic
6. **Recommended Next Steps**

### Audit Procedure

1. Check veto items first (T04, C01, R10)
2. Score all 80 CORE-EEAT items
3. Calculate dimension and system scores
4. Apply content-type-specific weights
5. Identify top 5 priority improvements
6. Generate action plan (quick wins, medium effort, strategic)
